How posting uses this mapping

At day end, Account Posting (FD-37) turns the posted charges, taxes and payments of the business date into accounting entries using these mappings. Unmapped items appear as warnings on Setup Home and on the error list of FD-37, with a Fix link back to this screen.

← Back

Account mapping grid

Every charge code, tax, payment mode, allowance, paid-out, advance deposit, city ledger, paymaster, round-off and cash over / short item needs a finance account. Account posting (FD-37) cannot run while a posted item is unmapped.
