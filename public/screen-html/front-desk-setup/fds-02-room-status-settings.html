General rules

Rules the room board, check-in and night audit follow.

When on, only Vacant Inspected rooms are assignable at check-in
Statuses assignable at check-in *
Usually Vacant Inspected (and Vacant Clean if inspection is off)
Automatic status change at check-out
All occupied rooms need daily service
Approval by role in FDS-22
Reason from FDS-19 (type Out of order)
Who may set Out of order / Out of service *
Chief Engineer / Front Office Manager with reason and expected return date (GM approval document)
Compare desk and housekeeping status; shown in night audit step 3
Badges on the room board (FD-06)
Changes are written to the setup audit log (FDS-X07).
