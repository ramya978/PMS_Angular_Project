Categories & GL Mapping

STS-02
Items Categories Units Stores Levels Valuation & Close
All 17 sub-categories are mapped to an inventory and a consumption GL account (BR-02) Stock received is posted to the inventory account; stock issued is posted to the consumption account of the receiving department. The accounts come from the chart of accounts of General Ledger Setup (ACS). Sub-sub-categories use the accounts of their sub-category.
Becomes the middle part of the item codes: ST-KDP-001
A sub-category in use cannot be deleted, only renamed
The top level – every sub-category sits under one category
Add its sub-categories next, then the sub-sub-categories
Example: Beverage › Coca-Cola range › Diet Coke
Uses the GL accounts and cost type of its sub-category

Changing a GL account affects new documents only; posted journals keep the account they were posted with.
