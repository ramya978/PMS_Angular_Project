Bump ticket

Tap an item to start it, tap again when ready; Bump ticket marks every item ready and sends the ticket to the expo. Allergens are highlighted in red; cancelled lines are struck through.

System – what runs automatically on this screen
  • KT-X01 Ticket receiver – splits each KOT from the POS, room service and banquets to the stations by the routing of KTS-02 and shows cancellations and changes.
  • KT-X02 Timer and colour – colours tickets by waiting time and alerts the sous chef when a ticket turns red.
  • KT-X03 Printer fallback – when this screen is offline the KOT prints on KP-01 (backup of KTS-01).
