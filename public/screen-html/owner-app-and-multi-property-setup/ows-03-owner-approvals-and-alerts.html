How a request reaches the owner

A request first follows the hotel's own approval chain. When the General Manager approves it and the amount is above the owner limit, it goes on to the owner, who answers in the owner app.

Example from the Approvals inbox PR-2026-00412 · combi oven ₹6,85,000 – a capital item above ₹5,00,000, so the owner approves after the General Manager
  1. Head of departmentArun Prakash · approved 19-10
  2. Financial ControllerLakshmi Ishaan · approved 20-10
  3. General ManagerVikram Nathan · waiting, escalated
  4. OwnerMr. T. K. Varghese · next, within 48 h
Owner approvals appear in the owner app and in the Approvals inbox (DA-03); the approval types and the hotel limits are set in Approval Types & Escalation (DAS-01).

Approval types to the owner

For each approval type: always, above an amount, or never. The General Manager's limit of the hotel is shown for comparison. If the owner does not answer in time, a reminder is sent; nothing is approved automatically.
