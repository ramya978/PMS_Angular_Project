System – what runs automatically on this screen
  • CC-X02 · Potential consumption – Runs every night: portions sold × recipe quantity ÷ yield, by ingredient.
  • CC-X04 · Variance alert – Flags the ingredients above tolerance and tells the Executive Chef.
