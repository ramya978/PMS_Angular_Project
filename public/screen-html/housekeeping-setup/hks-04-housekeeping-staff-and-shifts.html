Help for this screen HKS-04
What it is for
The housekeeping profile of each staff member: role, skill level, languages, home section, default shift, mobile login and inspection right. Shift codes are on the second tab.
How it works
1. Press Edit profile on a row. 2. Change the role, skill, section or shift. 3. Save. Staff who joined or left are pulled from HR with Sync from HR.
Opened from
Housekeeping Setup Home (HKS-01) › Staff & shifts
Leads to
HKS-P02 Staff Housekeeping Profile, HKS-04-2 Shift codes, HR-02 Employee Master, Super Admin user management
Sends to
Duty roster (HK-07), room allocation (HK-04) and the phone app login
Note
Static screen (HTML and CSS only): it shows the layout, fields and sample data. Saving and live data come when the backend is connected.
