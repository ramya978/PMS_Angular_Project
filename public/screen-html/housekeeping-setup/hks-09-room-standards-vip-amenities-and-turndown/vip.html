Help for this screen HKS-09
What it is for
The amenities each VIP level receives in the room on arrival, and who prepares them (Housekeeping, or an order to F&B).
How it works
1. Press Edit standard on a line or Add amenity. 2. Choose the VIP level and the item. 3. Save – VIP standards wait for the General Manager’s approval.
Opened from
Housekeeping Setup Home (HKS-01) › Room standards
Leads to
HKS-P11 Amenity Standard Form, HKS-09-1 Room set-up, VIP preparations (HK-23)
Sends to
VIP preparations (HK-23), turndown (HK-13), Linen & Amenities expected consumption (LN) and the Housekeeping phone app
Note
Static screen (HTML and CSS only): it shows the layout, fields and sample data. Items come from the Linen & Amenities item master (LNS), (LNS-02). Saving and live data come when the backend is connected.
